BTN wants to bet most of their strong Kx for value, threshold here is K5-K3, mix in 50% checking to go into the check back line

The bluffs you pick, e.g random Jx and Tx hands should be the hands that have backdoor potential. In this case, the hands like J6s (without the backdoor flush draw) and T5s (without the backdoor flush draw) are actually near 100% checks. It is more important on this dry flop to have a hand that in the future will have cooler EV, more, than being able to fold out the floats on the turn. 

This is a common theme, that the hands without the backdoor prefer the check, although they still bet sometimes, e.g a hand like Q3s with no backdoor just reduces frequency of small bet from 70 to 50%.

The wrap hands do like betting a lot more and so do your bottom pairs, so J8 loves betting

And 87 doesn't bet as much as 76, protection is pretty key here
